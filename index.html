IMy Sign: Astrology & People

Astrology that remembers you.

Coming to iPhone · live at mysignapp.com

My Sign starts from your real birth chart and the real sky, calculated from your exact birth date, time and place. It scores the people in your life from both full charts, finds patterns in your own mood check-ins, and has an AI astrologer that remembers what you've told it. You can see everything it remembers, and forget any of it.

Screens captured from the iPhone build with a demo account. The people and check-ins shown are made up.

An assistant for law firms that asks first.
