My Sign

Coming to iPhone · Available now on the web

An astrology app built on your real birth chart. It scores the people in your life from both charts, finds patterns in your own mood check-ins, and has an AI astrologer that remembers what you've told it. You can see everything it remembers and delete any of it.

Belted

In development for iPhone · Preview on the web

A fantasy league for real life. Friends compete at lifting, reading, water, runs, steps and trivia, and every claim needs a receipt: a live photo or video shot in the app, never the camera roll. The league votes each one legit or cap. Challenge a friend to a duel, take the title belt for a category, and play the monthly Major.
