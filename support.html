If you need help right now: My Sign is not a crisis service, and these inboxes aren't watched around the clock. If you might hurt yourself or someone else, call your local emergency number. In the US, you can call or text 988 at any hour.

My Sign: common questions

Signing in. There's no password. Enter your email and we send a six-digit code. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then write to support@mysignapp.com.

Deleting your account. In the app, open Settings, then Delete everything. You confirm twice, and your account and all its data are deleted right away. It can't be undone.

Getting a copy of your data. Settings, then Download your data, then Export.

Subscriptions and refunds. If you subscribed through the App Store, Apple bills you: cancel or ask for a refund in your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting your My Sign account does not cancel an App Store subscription. For anything else, see mysignapp.com/refunds.

The full guide is at mysignapp.com/support.
