Common questions

Signing in

There's no password. Enter your email and we send you a six-digit code. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete everything. Your account and all its data are deleted right away, and this can't be undone.

Getting a copy of your data

Settings, then Download your data, then Export.

Subscriptions and refunds

If you subscribed through the App Store, cancel or ask for a refund in your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. For anything else, see mysignapp.com/refunds.
