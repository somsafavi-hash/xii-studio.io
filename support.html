My Sign questions

Signing in

There's no password. Enter your email and we send you a six-digit code. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete everything. Your account and all its data are deleted right away, and this can't be undone.

Getting a copy of your data

Settings, then Download your data, then Export.

Subscriptions and refunds

If you subscribed through the App Store, cancel or ask for a refund in your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. For anything else, see mysignapp.com/refunds.

Belted questions

Signing in

There's no password, email or phone number. Your account is your name plus the phone you set it up on. To use a second phone, open the Me tab, then Account, then "Use Belted on another phone", and type the one-time code it shows on the new phone.

Reporting a post or a person

Open the menu on any receipt or chat message and choose Report. It disappears for you right away. You can also block a member. Photos and videos are also screened automatically when they're posted. The person who runs the league and XII Studios review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the terms, and remove the person who posted it.

Photos, videos and chat

Proof and chat photos and videos are shot live in the app; Belted can't read your photo library. Videos run up to 30 seconds. They are stored privately and shown only to the people in your league. Deleting a message, leaving a league or deleting your account deletes them.

Deleting your account

Me tab, then Account, then Delete my account. Your photos, videos and messages are deleted. Your past results stay in the league's history under "Deleted player". You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Money

Belted is free. It has no purchases, cash prizes, betting or entry fees.

Retrograde questions

Where the dates come from

The app calculates them on your phone from the planets' orbits and shows them in your time zone. A retrograde can start or end on a different calendar day than a website lists if that website uses another time zone.

Widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Retrograde. The Today widget is free. The other six widgets need Retrograde Pro, and show a lock until you unlock it. A widget can take a few minutes to update after you buy.

Alerts

Turn on Alerts in the You tab. If nothing arrives, check that notifications are allowed for Retrograde in the Settings app.

Money and refunds

Retrograde Pro is either a yearly subscription with a free first week, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The yearly plan renews automatically until you cancel. To cancel, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, at least a day before it renews. On a new phone, tap Restore purchase in the You tab to get Pro back without paying again. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
