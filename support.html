My Sign questions

Signing in. There's no password. Enter your email and we send a six-digit code. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then write to support@mysignapp.com.

Deleting your account. In the app, go to Settings, then Delete everything. You confirm twice, and your account and all its data are deleted right away. It can't be undone.

Getting a copy of your data. Settings, then Download your data, then Export.

Subscriptions and refunds. If you subscribed through the App Store, Apple bills you, so cancel or ask for a refund in your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting your My Sign account does not cancel an App Store subscription. For everything else, see mysignapp.com/refunds.
