My Sign questions

Signing in

There's no password. Enter your email and we send you a six-digit code. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete everything. Your account and all its data are deleted right away, and this can't be undone.

Getting a copy of your data

Settings, then Download your data, then Export.

Subscriptions and refunds

If you subscribed through the App Store, cancel or ask for a refund in your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. For anything else, see mysignapp.com/refunds.

Belted questions

Signing in

There's no password, email or phone number. Your account is your name plus the phone you set it up on. To use a second phone, open the Me tab, then Account, then "Use Belted on another phone", and type the one-time code it shows on the new phone.

Reporting a post or a person

Open the menu on any receipt or chat message and choose Report. It disappears for you right away. You can also block a member. The person who runs the league and XII Studios review reports, normally within 24 hours, and can remove content or remove someone from a league.

Deleting your account

Me tab, then Account, then Delete my account. Your photos, videos and messages are deleted. Your past results stay in the league's history under "Deleted player". You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Money

Belted is free. It has no purchases, cash prizes, betting or entry fees.
