Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This page covers xii-studio.io, the website of XII Studios LLC, Sunny Isles Beach, Florida, USA. The My Sign privacy policy covers the My Sign app on iPhone and the web.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, ads or tracking. Fonts and images are served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else to show it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the page you asked for, and may keep server logs for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Contact

About this page: som@xii-studio.io. About your My Sign data: support@mysignapp.com.
