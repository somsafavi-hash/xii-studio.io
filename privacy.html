Privacy

This website keeps nothing.

Last updated 10 November 2026

This page covers xii-studio.io, the website of XII Studios LLC, Sunny Isles Beach, Florida, USA. Our apps have their own policies: the My Sign privacy policy covers the My Sign app on iPhone and the web.

What this site collects

Nothing of its own. There are no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Fonts and images are served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else to show it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the page you asked for when you visit, and may keep server logs for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email us

We get your email address and what you wrote, and we use them only to reply. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message. Ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Links to other sites

Links to mysignapp.com, the mySign Shop on Shopify, TikTok, Instagram and GitHub take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this page: som@xii-studio.io. Questions about your My Sign data: support@mysignapp.com.
