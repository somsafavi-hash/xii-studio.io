Belted Privacy Policy

This explains what Belted collects, why, and what you can do about it. Belted is made by XII Studios LLC.

What we collect

  • Your name as you type it, and an account ID we generate. No email, phone number or password.
  • What you post: receipts (numbers, captions, live photos and videos), chat messages, votes, challenges and results.
  • Apple Health data, only if you choose to post it: today’s step count or running distance, read on your phone when you tap “Post from Apple Health”. Only that number is sent.
  • Basic technical logs such as request times and errors, kept for up to 30 days to keep the service running.
  • Activity counts: that a receipt was posted, a challenge sent or a league joined, with your account ID and the time. Not your name, your messages or your photos. Kept for up to 6 months.

How we use it

Only to run the game: showing your posts to the people in your leagues, working out scores and standings, and reviewing reports. We use the activity counts ourselves to see how leagues use Belted and what to improve; no outside analytics company gets them. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Health data is used only for the feature you asked for. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone outside your league.

Who sees it

The members of each league you’re in see what you post there. Photos and videos are stored privately and shown through links that expire. The person who runs a league and XII Studios can see reported content to review it. Our hosting provider, Amazon Web Services (United States), stores the data for us.

Camera and microphone

Belted uses your camera, and your microphone for videos, only while you are taking proof. It can’t read your photo library.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while your account exists. Leaving a league deletes your photos, videos and messages in that league. Deleting your account (Me tab → Account) does that everywhere and deletes the account. Scores stay in league history under “Deleted player”. You can also email us to ask for a copy or deletion.

Age

Belted is for adults 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you think we have, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
