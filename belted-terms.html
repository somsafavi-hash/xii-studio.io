Belted Terms of Use

Belted is a game you play with people you know. These terms are the rules for using it. By creating an account or joining a league you agree to them.

Who can use Belted

You must be 18 or older. One account per person. You’re responsible for what happens on the phones signed in to your account.

What you post

Your photos, videos, captions, messages and names stay yours. You give XII Studios permission to store them and show them to the people in your leagues so the app can work. Only post things you have the right to post, and only film people who are fine with it.

What isn’t allowed

  • Nudity or sexual content, hate, harassment, threats, or anything illegal.
  • Dangerous challenges, or challenges involving alcohol, drugs or weapons.
  • Betting money or anything of value. Belted has no cash prizes, pots or entry fees. Points and punishments are for fun.
  • Faking proof, pretending to be someone else, or trying to break or abuse the service.

Reports and removal

Anyone can report a post or message, and block another member. The person who runs a league and XII Studios review reports, normally within 24 hours, and can remove content or remove someone from a league. We can suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

Playing safely

Belted records what you say you did; it doesn’t coach you or check that a challenge is safe. Exercise and physical challenges carry risk. Know your limits, and talk to a doctor if you’re unsure. You take part at your own risk.

Leaving

You can leave a league or delete your account at any time from the Me tab. Deleting removes your photos, videos and messages. Your past results stay in the league’s history under “Deleted player”.
