Retrograde Privacy Policy

Retrograde doesn't collect anything about you. Retrograde is made by XII Studios LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It never sends anything to us or to anyone else, and it works without an internet connection.

How the app works

  • Dates and moon phases are calculated on your phone from the planets' orbits. The app doesn't ask for your location, birthday or birth details.
  • Two settings stay on your phone: whether alerts are on, and whether you have unlocked Retrograde Pro. The widgets read the second one so they know what to show. Deleting the app deletes both.
  • Alerts are scheduled on your phone. They aren't sent from a server, so we never know whether you have them on.

Purchases

Retrograde Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple sends us sales totals that don't identify you. For a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Children

Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything, this page will say so first and the app's listing on the App Store will be updated.
